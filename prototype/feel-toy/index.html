Skate Feel Toy

A street block with a stair set, rails, ledges and banks, and every movement number on a slider. Two sticks, laid out like Skate: the left stick skates, the right stick does tricks with Skate's flick-it motions. The camera follows behind you.

  • Left thumb steers: move from where you touched, pull down to powerslide to a stop, hold left or right in the air to spin.
  • Manuals: tip the right stick about halfway down (or halfway up for a nose manual). Manuals keep a combo going.
  • Walk gets you off the board to walk anywhere, including up stairs; Skate gets you back on.
  • Right thumb does tricks, also wherever you touch. Tap it to push (each tap is a kick). Pull down, flick up: ollie. Down, then up-right: kickflip. Down, then up-left: heelflip. Down, then left or right: pop shuvit. Start left, swing down, flick up-right: 360 flip. Push up and flick down for nollies.
  • Set spot drops a marker where you are, facing the way you're going; Return puts you back there, standing, to try the line again.
  • In the air the same motions are late flips. Hold the right stick in a direction to line the board up for a grind before you land on the rail.
