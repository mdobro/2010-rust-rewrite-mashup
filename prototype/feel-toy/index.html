Skate Feel Toy

One grey park and every movement number on a slider. Two sticks, laid out like Skate: the left stick skates, the right stick does tricks with Skate's flick-it motions. The camera follows behind you.

  • Left thumb steers. A stick appears wherever you touch the left side. You roll on your own; push up to go faster, pull down to brake, hold left or right in the air to spin.
  • Right thumb does tricks, also wherever you touch. Pull down, flick up: ollie. Down, then up-right: kickflip. Down, then up-left: heelflip. Down, then left or right: pop shuvit. Start left, swing down, flick up-right: 360 flip. Push up and flick down for nollies.
  • In the air the same motions are late flips. Hold the right stick in a direction to grab. Tap the right side for a quick ollie.
