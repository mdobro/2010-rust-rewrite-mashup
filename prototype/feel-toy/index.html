Skate Shooter Feel Toy

One grey park, a few targets, and every movement number on a slider. The only question: does skating while aiming feel good? Tricks reload your gun, but only when you land them.

  • Left thumb: touch anywhere on the left to get a stick. Steer to carve and spin. You roll on your own; push up to go faster, pull down to brake.
  • Right thumb: drag anywhere to aim. The trick pad in the corner is for swipes.
  • Ollie: on the pad, pull down then flick up. Flick up-left for a kickflip, up-right for a heelflip. A tap is a small ollie.
  • In the air: swipe the pad ← → ↑ ↓ for flips. Hold your thumb still to grab.
  • Shooting fires on its own when the reticle is on a target. Hold Fire and drag it to aim yourself.
  • Landing tricks reloads your gun. Turn the phone sideways for more room.
