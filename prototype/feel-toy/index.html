Skate Shooter Feel Toy

One grey park, a few targets, and every movement number on a slider. The only question: does skating while aiming feel good? Tricks reload your gun, but only when you land them.

  • Two sticks, like Skate. Left thumb steers: a stick appears wherever you touch. You roll on your own; push up to go faster, pull down to brake, hold left or right in the air to spin.
  • Right thumb is the flick-it stick, also wherever you touch. Pull down, flick up: ollie. Down then up-right: kickflip. Down then up-left: heelflip. Down then left or right: pop shuvit. Start left, swing down, flick up-right: 360 flip. Pull up and flick down for nollies.
  • In the air, the same motions are late flips. Hold the right stick in a direction to grab.
  • Shooting is automatic when the reticle is on a target. Hold Fire and drag it to aim yourself.
  • Shooting fires on its own when the reticle is on a target. Hold Fire and drag it to aim yourself.
  • Landing tricks reloads your gun. Turn the phone sideways for more room.
